<details><summary>How does adult growth hormone replacement dosing fundamentally differ from pediatric growth hormone replacement dosing?</summary>

Adult dosing is non-weight-based, starting at low fixed doses (0.1-0.3 mg/day) and titrated individually based on serum IGF-I concentrations and tolerance.

<details><summary>Why do adult females taking oral estrogen contraceptives or hormone replacement require higher growth hormone doses than males?</summary>

Oral estrogens undergo first-pass hepatic metabolism, inducing SOCS2 and suppressing hepatic GH-stimulated IGF-I production.
