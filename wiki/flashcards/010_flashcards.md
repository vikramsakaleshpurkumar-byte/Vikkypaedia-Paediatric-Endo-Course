<details><summary>What two biochemical parameters distinguish primary adrenal insufficiency from central/secondary adrenal insufficiency?</summary>

Markedly elevated plasma ACTH and hyperkalemia (with elevated renin/low aldosterone) are present in primary AI, whereas central AI features low/inappropriately normal ACTH with preserved mineralocorticoid secretion and normal potassium.

<details><summary>What peak serum cortisol threshold during a 250 mcg cosyntropin stimulation test is required to define normal adrenocortical reserve?</summary>

A peak serum cortisol of ≥ 18 mcg/dL (approx 500 nmol/L) at 30 or 60 minutes.

<details><summary>What is the initial intravenous hydrocortisone stress dose for an acute adrenal crisis in a school-aged child (ages 3–12 years)?</summary>

50 mg IV bolus (or 50 to 100 mg/m2 IV), accompanied by 20 mL/kg isotonic saline with 5–10% dextrose.

</details>

<details><summary>What is the physiological daily maintenance hydrocortisone dose in pediatric adrenal insufficiency?</summary>

8 to 10 mg/m2/day (divided into three doses, with the highest dose taken first thing in the morning).

</details>

<details><summary>What oral medication and standard starting dose are utilized for mineralocorticoid replacement in primary adrenal failure?</summary>

Fludrocortisone acetate at 0.05 to 0.2 mg orally once daily, adjusted by blood pressure and plasma renin activity.

</details>

<details><summary>What X-linked peroxisomal disorder causes progressive primary adrenal insufficiency and neurocognitive regression in boys aged 4 to 10 years?</summary>

X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy (X-ALD), caused by ABCD1 mutations leading to accumulation of very long-chain fatty acids (VLCFAs).

</details>

<details><summary>Why does cutaneous hyperpigmentation occur in primary adrenal failure but not in secondary adrenal failure?</summary>

Lack of cortisol negative feedback drives massive pituitary POMC cleavage into ACTH and alpha-MSH, which bind melanocortin 1 receptors (MC1R) on dermal melanocytes.

</details>
