<details><summary>What electrolyte and acid-base triad is classic for states of mineralocorticoid excess?</summary>

Hypertension, hypokalemia, and metabolic alkalosis, usually accompanied by suppressed plasma renin activity.

</details>

<details><summary>What paired screening test establishes the diagnosis of low-renin primary aldosteronism?</summary>

The plasma aldosterone concentration (PAC) to plasma renin activity (PRA) ratio, where PAC/PRA > 20 to 30 (with PAC > 15 ng/dL) is diagnostic.

</details>

<details><summary>What unique genetic mechanism causes Glucocorticoid-Remediable Aldosteronism (GRA / Familial Hyperaldosteronism Type I)?</summary>

An unequal crossing-over event creating a chimeric gene linking the ACTH-responsive CYP11B1 promoter to the aldosterone synthase (CYP11B2) coding sequence.

PDF p. 373
</details>

<details><summary>What enzyme protects the non-selective mineralocorticoid receptor from cortisol activation by rapidly converting cortisol to cortisone?</summary>

11-Beta-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase type 2 (11beta-HSD2), encoded by the HSD11B2 gene.

PDF p. 375
</details>

<details><summary>Why is Liddle syndrome completely unresponsive to spironolactone, and which drug class provides effective treatment?</summary>

The mutation causes constitutive ENaC channel surface overexpression independent of the mineralocorticoid receptor; treatment requires direct ENaC channel blockers (amiloride or triamterene).

PDF p. 375
</details>

<details><summary>What clinical differences distinguish autosomal dominant Renal PHA1 from autosomal recessive Systemic PHA1?</summary>

Renal PHA1 (MR mutation) is restricted to the kidney and improves spontaneously with age; Systemic PHA1 (ENaC subunit mutations) affects sweat, salivary, and lung epithelia, causing severe lifelong salt wasting.

</details>

<details><summary>What common pediatric clinical scenario produces transient secondary pseudohypoaldosteronism (PHA3) in young infants?</summary>

Acute pyelonephritis (urinary tract infection) associated with underlying congenital obstructive uropathy (e.g., posterior urethral valves or hydronephrosis).
