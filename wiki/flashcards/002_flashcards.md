<details><summary>What discordant biochemical profile distinguishes classic growth hormone insensitivity from growth hormone deficiency?</summary>

Elevated or normal circulating GH levels combined with profoundly depressed or undetectable serum IGF-I and IGFBP-3.

PDF p. 72
</details>

<details><summary>Why is serum growth hormone-binding protein (GHBP) undetectable in classic Laron syndrome?</summary>

GHBP is generated by proteolytic cleavage of the extracellular domain of the GHR; mutations affecting the extracellular domain prevent GHBP production and release into the circulation.

PDF p. 72
</details>

<details><summary>What approved pharmacological agent is indicated for children with severe primary IGF-I deficiency due to GHR mutations?</summary>

Recombinant human IGF-I (mecasermin), dosed at 40 to 120 mcg/kg subcutaneously twice daily with meals.

PDF p. 76
</details>

<details><summary>What critical administration guideline must be followed with every mecasermin injection to prevent an acute medical emergency?</summary>

Mecasermin must be injected immediately before or after a meal or carbohydrate snack to prevent severe, life-threatening hypoglycemia.

PDF p. 81
</details>

<details><summary>Mutations in STAT5B cause severe growth failure combined with which distinct non-endocrine clinical feature?</summary>

Severe immune deficiency with eczema, recurrent pulmonary infections, and autoimmune lymphoproliferation, due to impaired regulatory T-cell signaling.

PDF p. 60
</details>

<details><summary>What molecular defect causes moderate short stature with markedly low IGF-I and IGFBP-3, but normal free IGF-I and no hypoglycemia?</summary>

IGFALS (acid-labile subunit) gene mutations, which disrupt formation of the 150-kDa circulating ternary complex but leave binary complexes intact.

PDF p. 61
</details>

<details><summary>What unexpected long-term metabolic finding has been documented in Ecuadorian cohorts with homozygous E180splice GHR mutations?</summary>

Near-total protection from malignant neoplasms (cancer) and clinical type 2 diabetes mellitus despite marked lifelong obesity.

PDF p. 63
</details>
