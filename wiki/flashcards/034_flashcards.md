<details><summary>What are the updated international consensus terms for central and nephrogenic diabetes insipidus?</summary>

Arginine Vasopressin Deficiency (AVP-D) for central DI, and Arginine Vasopressin Resistance (AVP-R) for nephrogenic DI.

</details>

<details><summary>What are the three distinct chronological phases of the triphasic response following pituitary neurosurgery?</summary>

Phase 1 (days 1-2): acute polyuria (axonal shock); Phase 2 (days 3-7): transient SIADH with hyponatremia (uncontrolled AVP leak from dying axons); Phase 3 (day 8+): permanent AVP-D.

PDF p. 926
</details>

<details><summary>What is the most frequent genetic defect in congenital AVP resistance (nephrogenic DI), and what is its inheritance?</summary>

Inactivating mutations in the AVPR2 gene (encoding the V2 vasopressin receptor), inherited in an X-linked recessive manner (>90% of congenital cases).

PDF p. 928
</details>

<details><summary>What circulating peptide biomarker has emerged as the gold standard to differentiate AVP deficiency from AVP resistance without prolonged water deprivation?</summary>

Plasma copeptin (the C-terminal fragment of the AVP precursor); basal levels >21.4 pmol/L diagnose AVP-R, while stimulated levels <4.9 pmol/L confirm AVP-D.

PDF p. 933
</details>

<details><summary>What critical administration rule must be observed when dosing desmopressin (DDAVP) to avoid severe water intoxication?</summary>

Each dose must be withheld until a distinct breakthrough period of polyuria occurs, verifying water excretion and preventing life-threatening hyponatremia.

PDF p. 937
</details>

<details><summary>What characteristic finding on T1-weighted cranial MRI is typically absent in patients with AVP deficiency?</summary>

The hyperintense 'posterior pituitary bright spot' (which represents stored phospholipid-vesicle bound vasopressin) is absent in AVP-D.

PDF p. 934
</details>

<details><summary>What pharmacological combination is used to treat congenital AVP resistance (nephrogenic DI)?</summary>

A low-sodium diet combined with a thiazide diuretic (hydrochlorothiazide) and either amiloride or indomethacin.

</details>
