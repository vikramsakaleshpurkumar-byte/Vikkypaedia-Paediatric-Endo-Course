<details><summary>What pathognomonic growth pattern distinguishes pediatric Cushing syndrome from simple nutritional/exogenous obesity?</summary>

Excessive weight gain accompanied by severe linear growth deceleration or arrest (in contrast to simple obesity, where height velocity is accelerated and children are tall for age).

<details><summary>What is the most frequent cause of endogenous Cushing syndrome in children under 5 years versus children older than 7 years?</summary>

In children <5 years, primary adrenal tumors (adenomas and carcinomas) predominate; in children >7 years, ACTH-secreting pituitary adenomas (Cushing disease) account for >75% to 85% of cases.

</details>

<details><summary>Name the three approved first-line screening tests used to establish pathological hypercortisolemia in children.</summary>

24-hour urinary free cortisol (UFC), late-night (23:00 h) salivary cortisol (on two separate nights), and the low-dose dexamethasone suppression test.

</details>

<details><summary>What serum cortisol cutoff following an overnight low-dose dexamethasone suppression test excludes Cushing syndrome with high sensitivity?</summary>

Serum cortisol < 1.8 mcg/dL (approx 50 nmol/L) measured at 08:00 h following dexamethasone administration.

</details>

<details><summary>What invasive procedure is the diagnostic reference standard for distinguishing pituitary Cushing disease from ectopic ACTH secretion when pituitary MRI is equivocal?</summary>

Bilateral inferior petrosal sinus sampling (BIPSS) with CRH stimulation, demonstrating an IPS-to-peripheral ACTH ratio ≥2.0 at baseline or ≥3.0 post-CRH.

</details>

<details><summary>What biochemical finding within 24 to 72 hours following transsphenoidal surgery indicates surgical cure of Cushing disease?</summary>

Profound transient central adrenal insufficiency with morning serum cortisol < 1.0 to 2.0 mcg/dL.

<details><summary>Which syndrome featuring primary pigmented nodular adrenocortical disease (PPNAD), cardiac myxomas, and spotty skin pigmentation is caused by PRKAR1A mutations?</summary>

Carney complex, an autosomal dominant disorder causing ACTH-independent Cushing syndrome with cyclical hypercortisolemia.
