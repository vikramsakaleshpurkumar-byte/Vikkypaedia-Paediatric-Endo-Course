<details><summary>What enzyme deficiency accounts for more than 90% to 95% of all cases of congenital adrenal hyperplasia?</summary>

21-Hydroxylase deficiency, caused by autosomal recessive mutations in the CYP21A2 gene on chromosome 6p21.3.

PDF p. 311
</details>

<details><summary>At what age do male infants with undiagnosed classic salt-wasting 21-hydroxylase deficiency typically present with acute crisis?</summary>

At 7 to 21 days (1 to 3 weeks) of life, presenting with poor feeding, vomiting, failure to thrive, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, and hypovolemic shock.

PDF p. 313
</details>

<details><summary>What primary analyte is measured on newborn dried blood spots to screen for classic 21-hydroxylase deficiency?</summary>

17-Hydroxyprogesterone (17-OHP), with cutoffs adjusted for gestational age and birth weight to reduce false-positive rates in premature infants.

PDF p. 314
</details>

<details><summary>Why is oral sodium chloride supplementation (1 to 2 g/day) specifically required in infants with classic salt-wasting CAH in addition to fludrocortisone?</summary>

Immature neonatal renal tubules have reduced sensitivity to mineralocorticoids and high fractional sodium excretion, requiring exogenous sodium supplementation until dietary sodium intake increases.

PDF p. 322
</details>

<details><summary>What benign tumor occurs in the testes of adolescent and adult males with classic CAH due to poor disease control?</summary>

Testicular Adrenal Rest Tumors (TART), which can cause testicular pain, obstruction of seminiferous tubules, and irreversible azoospermia.

PDF p. 326
</details>

<details><summary>Which rare form of CAH features severe virilization accompanied by low-renin hypertension and hypokalemia?</summary>

11-Beta-hydroxylase deficiency (CYP11B1), due to accumulation of 11-deoxycorticosterone (DOC), a potent mineralocorticoid agonist.

PDF p. 316
</details>

<details><summary>What newly characterized pathway accounts for persistent virilization in classic CAH despite suppression of conventional circulating androgens?</summary>

The alternative 'backdoor pathway' (generating 11-oxygenated androgens like 11-ketotestosterone and 11-hydroxytestosterone from 17-OHP).

PDF p. 317
</details>
