<details><summary>What is the earliest and most common circulating thyroid hormone alteration in non-thyroidal illness syndrome (NTIS)?</summary>

Low total and free triiodothyronine (T3) accompanied by elevated reverse T3 (rT3) ('low T3 syndrome'), caused by impaired 5'-deiodination of T4 to T3 and increased clearance to rT3.

PDF p. 415
</details>

<details><summary>Which deiodinase enzyme is pathologically upregulated in peripheral tissues during severe systemic illness, driving the conversion of T4 into inactive reverse T3?</summary>

Type 3 iodothyronine deiodinase (DIO3), which mediates inner-ring deiodination of T4 to rT3 and degrades active T3 to inactive 3,3'-T2.

PDF p. 416
</details>

<details><summary>How does serum reverse T3 (rT3) distinguish severe non-thyroidal illness syndrome from central (pituitary) hypothyroidism?</summary>

Reverse T3 is typically elevated or high-normal in NTIS (due to increased DIO3 activity and reduced rT3 clearance), whereas rT3 is low in central hypothyroidism (due to low substrate T4 secretion without peripheral shunting).

PDF p. 422
</details>

<details><summary>What transient biochemical phenomenon occurs in serum TSH during clinical recovery from critical non-thyroidal illness?</summary>

A transient rebound elevation of serum TSH (frequently 5-15 mIU/L) occurs as hypothalamic-pituitary suppression resolves, which normalizes spontaneously and should not be misdiagnosed as permanent primary hypothyroidism.

PDF p. 422
</details>

<details><summary>Is routine thyroid hormone replacement (T4 or T3) recommended for critically ill pediatric patients with low T3/T4 in NTIS?</summary>

No. Randomized controlled trials show no clinical benefit in mortality or ICU length of stay, and guidelines recommend against routine replacement therapy; management focuses on treating the underlying illness.

PDF p. 425
</details>

<details><summary>Which common intensive care medications directly suppress pituitary TSH secretion, exacerbating low TSH levels in critically ill children?</summary>

Dopamine infusions and high-dose exogenous glucocorticoids directly inhibit pituitary thyrotrope TSH release.

PDF p. 419
</details>

<details><summary>What is the clinical prognostic significance of a profoundly depressed free T4 level in a child with severe critical illness?</summary>

Profound reduction in free T4 correlates directly with illness severity, multi-organ failure, and increased risk of intensive care mortality.

</details>
