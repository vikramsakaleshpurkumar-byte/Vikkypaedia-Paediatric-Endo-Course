<details><summary>Why does maternal transmission of a GNAS mutation produce multihormone resistance in PHP-1A, whereas paternal transmission produces PPHP without hormone resistance?</summary>

GNAS undergoes tissue-specific maternal expression in the renal proximal tubule, thyroid, and pituitary; maternal mutations ablate Gs-alpha in these tissues, whereas paternal mutations leave the active maternal allele intact.

</details>

<details><summary>What physical features define the Albright Hereditary Osteodystrophy (AHO) phenotype?</summary>

Short adult stature, rounded facies, early-onset centripetal obesity, brachydactyly (shortening of 4th and 5th metacarpals/metatarsals, dimple sign), subcutaneous heterotopic ossifications, and variable cognitive impairment.

PDF p. 568
</details>

<details><summary>What circulating biochemical profile defines untreated pseudohypoparathyroidism type 1A (PHP-1A)?</summary>

Hypocalcemia, hyperphosphatemia, and markedly elevated intact parathyroid hormone (PTH) levels, reflecting end-organ renal proximal tubular resistance to PTH.

PDF p. 569
</details>

<details><summary>What clinical feature distinguishes Progressive Osseous Heteroplasia (POH) from the subcutaneous ossifications seen in classic PHP-1A?</summary>

POH features aggressive, progressive heterotopic ossification that penetrates deeply through the dermis into subcutaneous fat, skeletal muscle, tendons, and deep fascia, severely restricting joint mobility.

PDF p. 577
</details>

<details><summary>Which endocrine hormone axes besides PTH frequently exhibit hormone resistance in children with PHP-1A?</summary>

Thyroid-stimulating hormone (elevated TSH), growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH resistance with GH deficiency), and gonadotropins (LH/FSH resistance with delayed puberty/amenorrhea).

PDF p. 570
</details>

<details><summary>What active vitamin D medication is essential to treat PTH resistance in PHP-1A and PHP-1B, and what is its starting dosage?</summary>

Calcitriol (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D) at 20-40 ng/kg/day (typically 0.25 to 1.0 mcg daily in divided doses) combined with oral elemental calcium.

</details>

<details><summary>What consensus classification term established in 2016 unifies PHP, PPHP, POH, and acrodysostosis into a single diagnostic continuum?</summary>

Inactivating PTH/PTHrP signaling disorders (iPPSD).
