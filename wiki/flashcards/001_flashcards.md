<details><summary>What classic triad in a male neonate strongly suggests congenital anterior pituitary hormone deficiency?</summary>

Hypoglycemia, prolonged conjugated hyperbilirubinemia, and micropenis (with or without undescended testes).

<details><summary>Why is sex-steroid priming utilized prior to provocative GH stimulation testing in peripubertal children?</summary>

Peripubertal children with constitutional growth delay have transient physiological hyposecretion of GH; priming with estrogen or testosterone enhances pituitary somatotroph responsiveness and prevents false-positive failure on provocative tests.

</details>

<details><summary>What triad of findings on pituitary MRI defines the Pituitary Stalk Interruption Syndrome (PSIS)?</summary>

An interrupted or absent (thin) pituitary infundibular stalk, an ectopic posterior pituitary bright spot (typically at the median eminence), and severe anterior pituitary hypoplasia.

</details>

<details><summary>Mutations in which pituitary-specific transcription factor produce combined deficiencies of GH, TSH, and Prolactin while initially sparing ACTH and gonadotropins?</summary>

POU1F1 (also known as PIT1), whereas PROP1 mutations cause progressive deficiency of GH, TSH, PRL, LH/FSH, and eventually ACTH in adulthood.

<details><summary>What is the recommended initial daily starting dose of somatropin for pediatric isolated or combined GHD?</summary>

22 to 35 mcg/kg/day (0.16 to 0.24 mg/kg/week) administered as a daily subcutaneous injection at bedtime.

<details><summary>A 10-year-old child 2 months into somatropin therapy develops persistent morning headaches and diplopia. What complication must be evaluated by funduscopic examination?</summary>

Benign intracranial hypertension (pseudotumor cerebri), characterized by papilledema and elevated cerebrospinal fluid opening pressure, requiring temporary cessation or dose reduction.

<details><summary>What orthopedic condition should be suspected in a patient on somatropin who develops groin, hip, or referred knee pain with an antalgic limp?</summary>

Slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE), related to rapid chondrocyte proliferation and structural biomechanical stress at the proximal femoral growth plate.
