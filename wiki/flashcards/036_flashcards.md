<details><summary>What three major endocrine organ systems are characteristically affected in Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 1 (MEN1)?</summary>

Parathyroid glands (multigland hyperplasia), Pancreatic enteroneuroendocrine tumors (gastrinoma, insulinoma), and Pituitary adenomas (prolactinoma, somatotropinoma).

</details>

<details><summary>What genes are mutated in MEN1 and MEN2, and what are their respective molecular mechanisms?</summary>

MEN1 is caused by inactivating mutations in the MEN1 tumor suppressor gene (encoding menin); MEN2 is caused by activating gain-of-function mutations in the RET proto-oncogene.

PDF p. 958
</details>

<details><summary>Why must screening for pheochromocytoma always precede thyroid surgery in patients with MEN2A or MEN2B?</summary>

Induction of general anesthesia or neck surgery in a patient with an unmanaged pheochromocytoma triggers massive catecholamine release, precipitating lethal hypertensive crisis and cardiac arrest.

PDF p. 974
</details>

<details><summary>When should prophylactic total thyroidectomy be performed in an infant diagnosed with MEN2B carrying the RET M918T mutation?</summary>

Within the first year of life (optimally in the first months of life) due to the virulent, early metastatic spread of medullary thyroid carcinoma.

</details>

<details><summary>What is the most common functioning pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor (pNET) presenting in pediatric patients under 20 years with MEN1?</summary>

Insulinoma, presenting with neuroglycopenic hypoglycemia, fasting hyperinsulinism, and positive critical blood sample.

</details>

<details><summary>What tumor suppressor gene is mutated in 15-20% of Familial Isolated Pituitary Adenoma (FIPA) cases, predisposing to aggressive pediatric gigantism?</summary>

AIP (aryl hydrocarbon receptor-interacting protein) on chromosome 11q13.3.

</details>

<details><summary>What genetic duplication causes X-linked acrogigantism (X-LAG syndrome) presenting with extreme linear overgrowth in infants &lt;2 years?</summary>

Microduplication of chromosome Xq26.3 encompassing the GPR101 gene.
