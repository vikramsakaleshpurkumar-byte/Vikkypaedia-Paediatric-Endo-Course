<details><summary>What three cardinal clinical features comprise the diagnostic triad of Autoimmune Polyglandular Syndrome Type 1 (APS-1 / APECED)?</summary>

Chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis (CMC), chronic hypoparathyroidism, and primary adrenal insufficiency (Addison disease); diagnosis requires >=2 of 3 (or 1 in a sibling).

</details>

<details><summary>What gene is mutated in APS-1, and what is its physiological role in the immune system?</summary>

The AIRE gene on chromosome 21q22.3; it mediates ectopic expression of peripheral self-antigens in medullary thymic epithelial cells for negative selection of autoreactive T cells.

PDF p. 946
</details>

<details><summary>What neutralizing circulating autoantibodies are present in &gt;95-100% of APS-1 patients from early childhood, serving as a first-line diagnostic biomarker?</summary>

Autoantibodies against type I interferons, specifically anti-interferon-omega (IFN-omega) and anti-interferon-alpha2 (IFN-alpha2).

PDF p. 948
</details>

<details><summary>What endocrine components distinguish APS-2 (Schmidt syndrome) from APS-1?</summary>

APS-2 requires primary adrenal insufficiency plus autoimmune thyroid disease and/or type 1 diabetes, but characteristically lacks chronic candidiasis, hypoparathyroidism, and ectodermal dystrophy.

PDF p. 951
</details>

<details><summary>What classic triad of symptoms characterizes IPEX syndrome in male infants, and what gene is mutated?</summary>

Intractable secretory enteropathy (diarrhea), neonatal type 1 diabetes, and severe eczematous dermatitis, caused by hemizygous mutations in the FOXP3 gene.

PDF p. 953
</details>

<details><summary>What dental enamel abnormality is present in over 80% of children with APS-1?</summary>

Enamel hypoplasia, characterized by horizontal pitting, grooving, and thinning of the enamel of the permanent dentition.

PDF p. 947
</details>

<details><summary>When a patient presents with concurrent untreated primary adrenal insufficiency and severe hypothyroidism, which hormone must be replaced first?</summary>

Hydrocortisone MUST be initiated before or concurrently with levothyroxine; starting levothyroxine alone accelerates hepatic cortisol metabolism and precipitates fatal adrenal crisis.
