<details><summary>How does the annual rate of pancreatic beta-cell decline in youth-onset type 2 diabetes compare to adult-onset type 2 diabetes?</summary>

Youth-onset T2D exhibits a 20-35% annual loss of beta-cell function (~4 times faster than adults), leading to rapid treatment failure and early insulin dependence.

<details><summary>What laboratory findings define Hyperglycemic Hyperosmolar State (HHS) in pediatric patients with type 2 diabetes?</summary>

Severe hyperglycemia (>600 mg/dL), effective serum osmolality >320 mOsm/kg, and absence of significant ketoacidosis (venous pH >7.25-7.30, bicarbonate >15 mmol/L).

PDF p. 844
</details>

<details><summary>What is the initial medical therapy for an asymptomatic adolescent diagnosed with T2D, HbA1c 7.6%, and no ketosis?</summary>

Oral metformin (initiated at 500 mg daily, titrated to 2000 mg/day divided twice daily) combined with diet and lifestyle modifications.

</details>

<details><summary>When is initial basal insulin therapy mandated at the time of diagnosis in pediatric type 2 diabetes?</summary>

When blood glucose is >=250 mg/dL or HbA1c is >=8.5% at presentation, or whenever ketosis or acidosis is present.

<details><summary>Which GLP-1 receptor agonists are FDA-approved for glycemic control and weight reduction in youth aged &gt;=10 years with T2D?</summary>

Liraglutide (once daily SC), dulaglutide (once weekly SC), and exenatide extended-release (once weekly SC).

<details><summary>Why must pancreatic islet autoantibodies be checked in all overweight youth presenting with clinical features of T2D?</summary>

To exclude autoimmune type 1 diabetes (or latent autoimmune diabetes), which occurs in ~10-15% of obese youth and requires lifelong intensive insulin therapy.

PDF p. 841
</details>

<details><summary>At what point should screening for diabetic nephropathy and retinopathy begin in youth with type 2 diabetes?</summary>

At the time of initial diagnosis (and annually thereafter), because microvascular complications frequently develop prior to clinical detection in youth.
