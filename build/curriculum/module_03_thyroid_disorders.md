# Module 3 Blueprint: Pediatric Thyroid Disorders

## Module identity

- Stable module ID and title: `MOD-03-THYROID` — Pediatric Thyroid Disorders: Congenital, Autoimmune, Non-thyroidal Illness, Hormone Resistance, and Neoplasia
- Source chapter IDs and PDF pages: Chapters 14 to 18 (PDF pp. 382–483, Printed pp. 371–473)
- Audience and intended setting: Pediatric endocrinology fellows, general pediatricians, pediatric oncology fellows, and hospitalists.
- Need or problem addressed: Preventing severe neurocognitive impairment through immediate diagnosis and age-dosed levothyroxine therapy for congenital hypothyroidism; managing pediatric Graves' disease and Hashimoto thyroiditis; distinguishing euthyroid sick syndrome (NTIS) and avoiding unnecessary hormone therapy; recognizing genetic hormone resistance (RTH-beta, RTH-alpha, Allan-Herndon-Dudley); stratifying high malignancy risk in pediatric thyroid nodules and managing differentiated/medullary thyroid cancer.
- Prerequisites: Understanding of hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid (HPT) axis physiology, thyroid hormone synthesis and peripheral deiodination (DIO1, DIO2, DIO3), and basic thyroid ultrasonography.
- Estimated learning effort and basis: 4 to 5 hours of structured self-directed study across 5 source chapters, 25 pillars, 35 flashcards, and 25 authored single-best-answer MCQs.
- Teaching levels supported: Essentials, Clinical Application, and Deep Dive.
- Source edition/version: *Pediatric Endocrinology: A Practical Clinical Guide*, 4th Edition (2024), Springer Nature Switzerland AG. Editors: Sally Radovick & Madhusmita Misra.
- Evidence actually reviewed on / reviewer role / unresolved issues: Chapters 14–18 reviewed against AAP/ESPE Congenital Hypothyroidism Consensus Guidelines and American Thyroid Association (ATA) Pediatric Thyroid Nodule and Differentiated Thyroid Cancer Guidelines.
- Suggested review date: 2027.

## Guided sequence

- Opening problem or clinical hook: A 12-day-old infant is recalled for newborn screen TSH 78 mIU/L and venous free T4 0.3 ng/dL. What exact formulation, dosage, and dietary precautions are required to safeguard cerebral IQ potential?
- Prior-knowledge check: Embryology of thyroid migration (foramen cecum to pretracheal position), thyroid hormone feedback regulation, and synthesis pathways (NIS, TPO, TG, Pendrin).
- Unit sequence:
  1. Unit 1: Congenital Hypothyroidism: Dysgenesis vs Dyshormonogenesis & Screening (Ch 14)
  2. Unit 2: Autoimmune Thyroid Disease: Hashimoto Deceleration vs Graves' Acceleration (Ch 15)
  3. Unit 3: Non-thyroidal Illness Syndrome: Deiodinase Reprogramming in PICU (Ch 16)
  4. Unit 4: Resistance to Thyroid Hormone (THRB, THRA) & MCT8 Defect (Ch 17)
  5. Unit 5: Pediatric Thyroid Nodules, Differentiated Cancer, and MEN2 MTC (Ch 18)
- Application case: A 13-year-old girl with Graves' disease taking methimazole presents with high fever (39.5°C) and severe exudative pharyngitis. What immediate life-saving action is mandated?
- Common error: Prescribing propylthiouracil (PTU) to a child with hyperthyroidism (black-box fatal liver failure), or giving levothyroxine to a critically ill child with euthyroid sick syndrome.
- Summary and retrieval practice: Revisit the 5 core pillars of Chapters 14 and 15; test active recall with flashcards `fc-014-1` through `fc-015-7`.
