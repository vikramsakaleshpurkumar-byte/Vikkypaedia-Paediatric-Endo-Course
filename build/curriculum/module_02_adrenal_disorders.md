# Module 2 Blueprint: Pediatric Adrenal Disorders

## Module identity

- Stable module ID and title: `MOD-02-ADRENAL` — Pediatric Adrenal Disorders: Insufficiency, Hyperplasia, Cushing, and Mineralocorticoids
- Source chapter IDs and PDF pages: Chapters 10 to 13 (PDF pp. 276–380, Printed pp. 263–368)
- Audience and intended setting: Pediatric endocrinology fellows, pediatric intensivists, emergency physicians, pediatric residents, and hospitalists.
- Need or problem addressed: Rapid identification of life-threatening acute adrenal crisis and congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) salt-wasting shock; distinguishing primary from central adrenal insufficiency; managing steroid maintenance and stress dosing; diagnosing Cushing syndrome; evaluating endocrine hypertension and mineralocorticoid disorders.
- Prerequisites: Knowledge of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, adrenal steroidogenesis pathway, aldosterone physiology, and renin-angiotensin system.
- Estimated learning effort and basis: 3 to 4 hours of structured self-directed study across 4 source chapters, 20 pillars, 28 cards, and 20 authored single-best-answer MCQs.
- Teaching levels supported: Essentials, Clinical Application, and Deep Dive.
- Source edition/version: *Pediatric Endocrinology: A Practical Clinical Guide*, 4th Edition (2024), Springer Nature Switzerland AG. Editors: Sally Radovick & Madhusmita Misra.
- Evidence actually reviewed on / reviewer role / unresolved issues: Chapters 10–13 reviewed against Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guidelines on Primary Adrenal Insufficiency, CAH, and Pediatric Cushing Syndrome.
- Suggested review date: 2027.

## Guided sequence

- Opening problem or clinical hook: A 10-day-old infant presents in shock with sodium 118 mEq/L, potassium 7.8 mEq/L, and ambiguous genitalia. What is the immediate life-saving medical management?
- Prior-knowledge check: Cortisol synthesis pathway (CYP21A2, CYP11B1, HSD3B2, StAR) and differentiating mineralocorticoid vs glucocorticoid actions.
- Unit sequence:
  1. Unit 1: Adrenal Insufficiency: Primary vs Central & Crisis Management (Ch 10)
  2. Unit 2: Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia: Molecular Pathology & Life-Span Management (Ch 11)
  3. Unit 3: Pediatric Cushing Syndrome: Growth Failure with Obesity & Differential Diagnosis (Ch 12)
  4. Unit 4: Mineralocorticoid Disorders & Endocrine Hypertension (Ch 13)
- Application case: A 12-year-old girl with primary autoimmune Addison disease presents with fever, vomiting, and BP 70/40 mmHg. Formulate her emergency fluid and hydrocortisone resuscitation, transition to oral stress dosing, and adjustments in fludrocortisone.
- Common error: Delaying hydrocortisone in a patient in septic shock or suspected adrenal crisis while waiting for laboratory confirmation, or starting thyroid hormone before cortisol replacement.
- Summary and retrieval practice: Review 5 core pillars of Chapter 10 and 11; test recall with flashcards `fc-010-1` through `fc-011-7`.
